# Go Client Design

**Status: historical design/planning note; its referenced `?extended=true` mode was later removed.** The design proposed a minimal public Go client that negotiated archive snapshots, decoded and filtered history, buffered live events, and cut over without gaps while emitting at least once. It decided on eager delivery without tombstone suppression, kept overlay/tombstone internals unexported, and used a JSONL live buffer with periodic fsync. References were `docs/README.md`, `internal/client/`, `segment/`, `internal/manifest/plan.go`, `internal/oracle/`, and `testing/mutation/RESULTS.md`.
