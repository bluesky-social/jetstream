# Subscribe v2 lexicon rename

The canonical v2 subscription lexicon is
`network.bsky.jetstream.subscribeEvents`, served at
`/xrpc/network.bsky.jetstream.subscribeEvents` and defined by
`lexicons/network/bsky/jetstream/subscribeEvents.json`.

This name supersedes `network.bsky.jetstream.subscribe` from the earlier
proposal-0015 design note. That note remains unchanged as a write-once record
of the design at the time.
