# Reviewer’s Guide: Drop Client Tombstones + Paginated Backfill

This historical review guide explained the shipped removal of client tombstone hiding, paginated archive backfill without a client buffer, one-based sequences, stricter cursor handling, and sentinel collection markers for DID-level tombstones. It directed reviewers to the planner/client cutover and marker-index invariants, plus oracle convergence scenarios. Key references were `segment/sentinel.go`, `internal/manifest/plan.go`, `internal/client/engine.go`, `internal/subscribe/cursor.go`, `docs/README.md`, `specs/oracle.md`, and `internal/oracle/`.
