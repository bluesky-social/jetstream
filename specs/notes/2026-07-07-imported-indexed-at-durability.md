# Imported `indexed_at` Durability: Rule Map Stamped at Ingest

**Status: decided design, not yet implemented; tracking [issue #269](https://github.com/bluesky-social/jetstream/issues/269).** The decision was to persist imported-indexed-at rules and stamp matching events during live/backfill ingest, while import updated the rule map and existing segments; compaction and resync would preserve the stamped value. The note accepted bounded lookup/cache and rule-lifecycle limitations rather than re-running imports on every new event. References were implementing [PR #275](https://github.com/bluesky-social/jetstream/pull/275), directly relevant crash/re-backfill [issue #262](https://github.com/bluesky-social/jetstream/issues/262), `docs/README.md`, `specs/notes/2026-07-01-timestamp-import-design.md`, `internal/ingest/live/events.go`, `internal/ingest/backfill/handler.go`, `internal/timestamp/bucket.go`, and `specs/gotchas.md`.
