# Imported `indexed_at` Durability: Rule Map Stamped at Ingest

**Status: decided design, not yet implemented; tracking issue #269.** The decision was to persist imported-indexed-at rules and stamp matching events during live/backfill ingest, while import updated the rule map and existing segments; compaction and resync would preserve the stamped value. The note accepted bounded lookup/cache and rule-lifecycle limitations rather than re-running imports on every new event.
