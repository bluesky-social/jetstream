# Readable Log (#248): Design and Implementation Plan

**Status: draft for review; implementation was not to start until approved.** The design proposed migrating live cursor persistence to durable-batch hooks, unifying sync and async flush behind one pipeline, adding a writer-owned ring and durable watermark, then repointing subscribers and deleting the duplicate hot-ring/sink seam. Durability and cursor semantics were to remain unchanged, with oracle and mutation gates for each stage. References were `specs/notes/2026-07-05-readable-log-writer-design.md`, `internal/ingest/writer.go`, `internal/ingest/readlog.go`, `internal/subscribe/`, `internal/jetstreamd/runtime.go`, and `internal/oracle/`.
