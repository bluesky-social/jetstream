# PDS-Direct Backfill Design (`listHosts` → per-PDS `listRepos`)

**Status: implemented 2026-08-03; production-scale tuning remained operational validation.** The design moved host discovery and multi-host scheduling into atmos: enumerate relay `listHosts`, crawl each PDS’s `listRepos`, persist host-aware progress, validate hostnames against SSRF, and preserve at-least-once repo completion and durability behavior. Jetstream changes covered the backfill store, bootstrap and merge discovery, failed-repo retry, simulator/oracle coverage, configuration, and observability.
