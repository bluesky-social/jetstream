# Performance Exploration — Server + Client on cpu2-pop3 (2026-07-08)

**Status: historical measurement report; initially no issues were filed.** Measurements found saturated subscriber disconnects, resident block blooms as a major RSS driver, duplicated cold-path encoding, hot fanout lock costs, and severe WAN/RTT amplification for block-mode and single-stream segment downloads; ingest and negotiation were generally not bottlenecks. The addendum records block parallelism via [issue #292](https://github.com/bluesky-social/jetstream/issues/292)/[PR #293](https://github.com/bluesky-social/jetstream/pull/293) and striped 16 MiB range downloads via [issue #296](https://github.com/bluesky-social/jetstream/issues/296)/[PR #298](https://github.com/bluesky-social/jetstream/pull/298) as implemented on 2026-07-09, while streaming and shared encodes remained opportunities and bloom sizing became follow-up [issue #302](https://github.com/bluesky-social/jetstream/issues/302). References were `internal/subscribe/`, `internal/ingest/readlog.go`, `internal/client/downloader.go`, `segment/inspect.go`, and `specs/gotchas.md`.
