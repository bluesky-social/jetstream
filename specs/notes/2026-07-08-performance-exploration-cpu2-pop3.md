# Performance Exploration — Server + Client on cpu2-pop3 (2026-07-08)

**Status: historical measurement report; initially no issues were filed.** Measurements found saturated subscriber disconnects, resident block blooms as a major RSS driver, duplicated cold-path encoding, hot fanout lock costs, and severe WAN/RTT amplification for block-mode and single-stream segment downloads; ingest and negotiation were generally not bottlenecks. The addendum records striped 16 MiB range downloads as implemented in #296 on 2026-07-09, while resume, streaming, shared encodes, and bloom changes remained opportunities. References were `internal/subscribe/`, `internal/ingest/readlog.go`, `internal/client/downloader.go`, `segment/inspect.go`, and `specs/gotchas.md`.
