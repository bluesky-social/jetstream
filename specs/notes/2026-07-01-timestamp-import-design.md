# Timestamp Import + Witnessed/Indexed Timestamp Rename

**Status: implementation complete.** The design retained two timestamp columns, renamed their semantics to witnessed and imported indexed time, and resolved wire `time_us` as imported-when-present otherwise witnessed; sentinel zero represented no import without changing the on-disk format. It implemented a bearer-gated online CSV job using a disk-backed, crash-safe `segment.Patch`/compaction-pass pipeline keyed by AT URI, with bounded row rejection and no dry-run or provenance field. References were [issue #193](https://github.com/bluesky-social/jetstream/issues/193), `docs/README.md`, `segment/patch.go`, `internal/timestamp/`, `internal/importer/`, and `cmd/jetstream/main.go`.
