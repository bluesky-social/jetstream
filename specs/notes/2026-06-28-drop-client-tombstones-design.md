# Simplify Backfill: Drop Client Tombstones + Paginated, Bufferless Cutover

**Status: historical design/plan; later implementation details superseded parts of the original proposal.** The design deliberately relaxed unilateral completeness: clients would emit matching events without read-time tombstone suppression and achieve complete mirrors through cooperative filters, while account/identity/sync events remained mandatory. Its original DID-tombstone snapshot was implemented then reverted; reserved DID-marker sentinel collections became the shipped mechanism ([issue #175](https://github.com/bluesky-social/jetstream/issues/175)), alongside paginated bufferless cutover and one-based sequences.
