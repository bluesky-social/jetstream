# Simplify Backfill: Drop Client Tombstones + Paginated, Bufferless Cutover

**Status: historical design/plan; later implementation details superseded parts of the original proposal.** The design deliberately relaxed unilateral completeness: clients would emit matching events without read-time tombstone suppression and achieve complete mirrors through cooperative filters, while account/identity/sync events remained mandatory. Its original DID-tombstone snapshot was implemented then reverted; reserved DID-marker sentinel collections became the shipped mechanism (issue #175), alongside paginated bufferless cutover and one-based sequences. References were `docs/README.md`, `segment/sentinel.go`, `internal/client/`, `internal/manifest/plan.go`, `internal/subscribe/`, and `internal/oracle/foldconvergence_gate_test.go`.
